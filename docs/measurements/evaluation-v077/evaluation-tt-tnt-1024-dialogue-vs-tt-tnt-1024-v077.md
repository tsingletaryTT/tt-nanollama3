## The noise floor

The floor is the **seed-only control**: two runs identical in everything but the RNG seed. Whatever it moves, a seed moves. Derived at runtime (`derived-live`) from `docs/measurements/behaviour-tt-tnt-v3-vs-tt-tnt-v5-setB.json` and `artifacts/checkpoints-tt-tnt-v3/val_losses.jsonl`, `artifacts/checkpoints-tt-tnt-v5/val_losses.jsonl`, never hardcoded.

- **loss floor:** sd of the seed control's per-checkpoint differences = **0.1944 nats** (mean 0.0413, sign 8/22 negative). The *spread*, not the mean: that control's mean delta is near zero precisely because its sign wanders, so the spread is what a candidate has to beat.
- **the rule:** a delta at or below **1.2x** the floor is `NOT INTERPRETABLE`, *whatever its confidence interval says*. A −0.041 register delta at 1.03x the floor cleared its CI, was written up, and was refuted by exactly this control; two collapse signals at 1.01x and 1.05x were correctly excluded on the same rule.
- **and the mirror-image rule:** a large ratio over a tiny denominator that cannot clear its own paired minimum-detectable difference is `below paired detection`, not a finding. Both gates have to pass.

## What would refute this

- A **seed replicate** of either arm. Every claim above rests on one run per arm; the floor is one seed pair, and a second pair could widen it.
- A **matched-window** re-measurement of anything quoted here from a different window. Where the two models' training windows differ, the trajectory comparison is refused rather than estimated, and that refusal is not a formality.
- The other frozen prompt set. A behavioural effect that appears on set B and not on set A is a fact about the sets as much as about the models.
